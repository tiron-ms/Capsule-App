# CAPSULE

> **Tactile Digital Time Vaults.**  
> Lock memories, media, and messages behind time—sealed with industrial precision and zero early access.

---

## Overview

**Capsule** is a minimalist, tactile time-capsule experience built around permanence, anticipation, and privacy. Designed in a stark **Cold Industry / Neon Pulse** aesthetic, it transforms the act of saving digital memories into a deliberate physical ritual. 

Whether locking away personal reflections for your future self or creating shared "blind-drop" vaults with friends, every capsule remains strictly encrypted and inaccessible until the exact countdown hits zero.

---

## Core Experience

### 1. The Sealing Ritual (Chamber Mechanics)
Saving a memory shouldn't feel like clicking a mundane form button. Sealing a capsule is an interactive, tactile sequence inspired by heavy mechanical vaults:
* **Hold-to-Seal Interaction:** Press and hold to draw your photos, voice notes, and text into the vault core.
* **Mechanical Locking Sequence:** As pressure is maintained, heavy concentric bolts slide shut around the chamber with synchronized tactile feedback.
* **Monolithic Seal:** Upon reaching 100%, the central locking disc rotates into place with an electric lime pulse (`#00FF66`), rendering the vault sealed until its scheduled unlock timestamp.
* **Reverse Unlock Sequence:** When the timer expires, the mechanism runs in reverse—bolts retract, the disc unlocks, and the contents are revealed.

### 2. True Cryptographic Privacy
* **Zero-Peek Guarantee:** Contents are encrypted directly on your device at the moment of sealing. 
* **Time-Locked Access:** Media and messages remain obscured behind frosted glass and cryptographic locks—impossible to preview or bypass before the countdown finishes.

### 3. Shared Vaults & Blind Drops (`$Cashtag`)
* **Frictionless Invites:** Connect with friends using lightweight `$cashtag` identifiers—no bloated profiles or unnecessary friction.
* **Blind-Drop Collaboration:** Everyone invited to a shared capsule can contribute photos and notes before the seal closes, yet **nobody can see what others uploaded** until the shared timer expires for everyone simultaneously.

## Design Philosophy: Cold Industry / Neon Pulse

* **Surface & Depth:** Deep matte obsidian (`#080808`), dark graphite modules (`#121212`), and crisp hairline steel borders (`#2A2A2A`).
* **Energy & State:** High-contrast electric lime (`#00FF66`) signals active sealing energy and locked states, paired with electric cyan (`#00E5FF`) for active countdowns and collaborative vaults.
* **Typography:** Utilitarian monospaced lettering for live countdowns, vault statuses (`SEALED_`, `LOCKED_`), and `$cashtag` identifiers, balanced with clean sans-serif body text.

## Vault States

| State | Visual Indicator | Access Level |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`DRAFT_`** | Open chamber aperture | Editable; ready to load media and set unlock date |
| **`SEALED_`** | Monolithic closed vault + live countdown | Strictly locked; encrypted payload inaccessible |
| **`READY_`** | Pulsing neon seam + active unlock prompt | Timer expired; ready for reverse-mechanism reveal |
| **`OPENED_`** | Retracted bolts + decrypted archive | Full access to personal or group memories |
